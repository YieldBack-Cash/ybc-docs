---
title: The exchange rate
description: The monotonic, maturity-locked rate that every mint and redemption depends on.
icon: Gauge
---

How much the underlying asset one vault share is worth, read from the vault, and
fed to every mint and redeem. If a share is worth 1.05
USDC, depositing 100 shares mints 105 PT and 105 YT.

> It starts at the vault's rate, only ever moves **up**, and **freezes at
> maturity**.

**Live.** Each operation takes `max(stored, vault_rate)`. If the vault dips, the
stored value holds where it was.

**At maturity.** It locks at whatever value it last reached.

## Why it matters

- **PT** redeems at `max(live_rate, locked_rate)`. If the vault grew, you get
  fewer shares worth more, with the difference kept as
  protocol surplus. If it fell, your share count is capped at what the locked rate implies,
  so the shortfall lands on you.
- **YT** captures exactly the appreciation between deposit and maturity.

<Callout type="warn">
The rate is only as honest as the vault reporting it. YBC confines the blast
radius to one market rather than proving vault honesty on-chain. See
[Risks](/concepts/risks).
</Callout>
