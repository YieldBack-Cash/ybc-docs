Three token types move through every market.

| Symbol | Name | What it is |
|--------|------|------------|
| **V** | Vault shares | The yield-bearing collateral, a 4626-style vault share token (e.g. a Blend vault share). |
| **PT** | Principal Token | A zero-coupon bond, claim on principal. Denominated in the base asset: 1 PT redeems for 1 unit of it **after** maturity. The fixed-yield leg. |
| **YT** | Yield Token | A claim on all variable yield the underlying V accrues until maturity. The variable-yield leg. |

## The conservation identity

The protocol maintains one identity at all times:

```
depositing V      →  mints equal amounts of PT and YT
PT + YT together  →  always redeemable back into V (before maturity)
```

Deposits mint PT and YT in **equal** amounts, and recombining burns them in equal
amounts. Outside of post-maturity redemption, PT and YT supply always move
together.

That amount is the deposit's **asset value**, not its share count. Shares are what
you hand over; the base asset is what PT and YT are denominated in.

**Example.** A vault share is worth 1.05 USDC. You deposit 100 shares, and are
minted 105 PT and 105 YT.

## What each token is worth

- **PT** approaches face value as maturity nears and trades below it before then.
  The discount is the fixed rate. Lock in your implied rate by buying PT tokens.
- **YT** is worth the yield still expected between now and maturity. It decays to
  zero at maturity. You can buy exposure to the yield of a much larger position,
  which is what makes YT a leveraged rate bet.

<Callout type="info">
The AMM's only reserves are PT and V. YT is synthesized from the `PT + YT = V` identity.
Mints and trades are settled atomically. See [Flash swaps](/protocol/flash-swaps).

</Callout>
