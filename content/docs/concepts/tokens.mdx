Three token types move through every market.

| Symbol | Name | What it is |
|--------|------|------------|
| **V** | Vault shares | The yield-bearing asset you bring in — a 4626-style vault share token (e.g. a Blend vault share). This is the unit of value that enters and leaves the protocol. |
| **PT** | Principal Token | A zero-coupon claim on principal. Redeemable 1:1 for face value in V **after** maturity. The fixed-yield leg. |
| **YT** | Yield Token | A claim on all variable yield the underlying V accrues until maturity. The variable-yield leg. |

## The conservation identity

The protocol maintains one identity at all times:

```
depositing V      →  mints equal amounts of PT and YT
PT + YT together  →  always redeemable back into V (before maturity)
```

Deposits mint PT and YT in **equal** amounts, and recombining burns them in equal
amounts. Outside of post-maturity redemption, PT and YT supply always move
together.

## What each token is worth

- **PT** approaches face value as maturity nears and trades below it before then.
  The discount *is* the fixed rate — lock it in by buying, and nothing the vault
  does afterwards changes your payout.
- **YT** is worth the yield still expected between now and maturity. It decays to
  zero at maturity, having paid out along the way. A small amount of V buys
  exposure to the yield of a much larger position, which is what makes YT a
  leveraged rate bet.

<Callout type="info">
YT is never held by a market's AMM. It is created and destroyed on demand via
flash swaps against PT — see [Flash swaps](/protocol/flash-swaps).
</Callout>

## Token behaviour

**PT** is a standard token. Mint and burn are restricted to its market's yield
manager; transfer, approve and `transfer_from` behave normally.

**YT** additionally tracks accrued yield per holder and distributes it on
`claim_yield`. It deliberately **omits allowance flows** — `approve`,
`transfer_from` and `burn_from` are unsupported and will panic. Move YT with a
direct `transfer`.
