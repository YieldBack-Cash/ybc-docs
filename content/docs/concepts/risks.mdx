**The vault behind a market is the main thing you are trusting, and no part of
YBC verifies it.**

A market can be created around any vault by anyone. Nothing in the contracts
checks that a vault is honest, solvent, or run by people you would lend to. A
market existing is not a market being vetted.

## Why it matters

The vault reports its own share value, and YBC prices every mint and redemption
through that number. A vault that reports dishonestly, loses value, or charges a
fee on the way out leaves PT worth more than the assets backing it.

There is also no way back. Markets are immutable and there is no pause switch, so
if a vault turns bad there is no admin to freeze it and no upgrade to fix it.

## What that means for you

**Vet the vault before you deposit.**

The one thing YBC does guarantee is containment: markets share no state, so a bad
vault only reaches the people who deposited into *its* market.

For how the protocol calls a vault, and which of its guarantees are assumed
rather than checked, see [Vault integration](/protocol/vault-integration).
