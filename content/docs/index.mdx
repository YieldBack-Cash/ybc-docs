**YieldBack.Cash (YBC)** is a Soroban protocol on Stellar for trading interest rate
derivatives. It takes a yield-bearing asset and splits it into two tradable halves:
a fixed-rate leg and a variable-rate leg.

## The core idea

Deposit vault shares — any 4626-style yield-bearing token, such as a Blend vault
share — and receive equal amounts of two tokens:

| Token | Name | What it entitles you to |
|-------|------|-------------------------|
| **PT** | Principal Token | The principal, redeemable at face value after maturity. A zero-coupon claim: buy it below face value and your return is locked in the moment you buy. |
| **YT** | Yield Token | All variable yield the deposit accrues until maturity. Leveraged, directional exposure to the interest rate itself. |

Held together, PT and YT are always redeemable back into the original vault
shares. Held apart, they are two different bets on the same rate.

```
deposit vault shares  →  mint equal PT + YT
PT + YT together      →  redeem back to vault shares (before maturity)
```

## Who it's for

<Cards>
  <Card title="Fixed yield seekers" icon={<span>🔒</span>}>
    Buy PT at a discount and hold to maturity. Your rate is fixed at purchase and
    is not affected by what the underlying vault does afterwards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rate speculators" icon={<span>📈</span>}>
    Buy YT to take amplified exposure to a vault's future yield, or sell it to
    hedge yield you already earn elsewhere.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Liquidity providers" icon={<span>💧</span>}>
    Supply PT and vault shares to a market's AMM and earn trading fees on both
    legs.
  </Card>
</Cards>
