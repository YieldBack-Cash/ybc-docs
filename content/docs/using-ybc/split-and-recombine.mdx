The two operations that create and destroy PT/YT pairs. Both are exact inverses
of each other and both go through the market's yield manager.

## Split — deposit V, receive PT + YT

```
YieldManager::deposit(from, shares_amount)
```

Pulls `shares_amount` of vault shares into the yield manager's custody, then
mints `shares_amount × rate / 1e7` of **both** PT and YT to you.

You now hold the fixed leg and the variable leg separately, and can sell either
one while keeping the other. Selling the YT locks in a fixed rate on the whole
position; selling the PT leaves you with pure rate exposure.

<Callout type="warn">
`deposit` is rejected after maturity. Once a market matures there is no new yield
to split.
</Callout>

## Recombine — PT + YT back to V

```
YieldManager::redeem_combined(from, amount)
```

Burns `amount` PT and `amount` YT together and returns `amount × 1e7 / rate`
vault shares — the exact inverse of the split.

This is disabled at maturity. After maturity, PT is redeemed on its own via
[`redeem_principal`](/using-ybc/maturity) and YT has nothing left to claim.

## Doing it in the base asset

If you hold the vault's underlying asset (USDC, say) rather than vault shares,
`zap_asset_for_split` and `zap_split_for_asset` wrap these two calls in a vault
deposit or redeem so you never touch shares at all. See [Zaps](/using-ybc/zaps).
